This study analyzes Rudy Gobert’s defensive impact in the playoff series between the Minnesota Timberwolves and the Denver Nuggets in 2024 and 2026, with the aim of comparing his effectiveness and evolution over time. Through the analysis of Denver’s shot selection, shot distance distribution, and midrange shooting probability, the study evaluates how Gobert’s presence influenced the Nuggets’ offensive decisions in the two series. The comparison also highlights possible tactical adjustments made by Denver and changes in Minnesota’s defensive system between 2024 and 2026.
